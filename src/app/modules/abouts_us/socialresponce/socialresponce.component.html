SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY
Vedha is dedicated to improving environmental sustainability and enhancing the welfare of our employees, customers, and the broader community.
Our commitment to our employees extends beyond their professional endeavors—we also prioritize their personal well-being. We ensure that they receive living wages and have access to comprehensive medical coverage.
Numerous migrant workers from neighboring regions seek opportunities in our region due to its attractiveness. Unfortunately, many of these individuals lack legal documentation, leaving them susceptible to exploitation and mistreatment. Vedha plays a crucial role in facilitating the legal documentation of these migrant workers upon their employment, thereby ensuring they are treated fairly and receive appropriate compensation.
