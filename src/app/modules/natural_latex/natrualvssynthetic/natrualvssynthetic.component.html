NATURAL VERSUS SYNTHETIC LATEX
Natural latex is a milky substance derived from the rubber tree, Hevea brasiliensis. It consists primarily of water, latex, proteins, and various organic compounds. This material is known for its elasticity and resilience, making it a valuable resource in manufacturing products like gloves, mattresses, and balloons.
Synthetic latex typically contains a blend of styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR) or polybutadiene rubber (PBR) along with other synthetic chemicals. Its composition often includes petroleum-based derivatives like styrene and butadiene, formulated to mimic the properties of natural latex.
