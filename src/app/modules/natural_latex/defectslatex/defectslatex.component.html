SUSTAINABLE HARVESTING OF LATEX
Protein Allergies: Natural latex contains proteins that can trigger allergic reactions in some individuals. Latex allergies range from mild skin irritation to severe allergic reactions, including anaphylaxis.
Vulcanization Issues: During the processing of natural latex, vulcanization is often used to improve its strength and elasticity. Improper vulcanization can lead to defects such as insufficient curing, which may result in weakened or brittle latex products.
Latex Odor: latex can emit a distinct odor, which might be unpleasant to some people. The smell is caused by the compounds present in latex and might persist in products made from it.
Poor Heat Resistance: latex has relatively lower heat resistance compared to synthetic materials. This can cause deformation or softening of latex-based products when exposed to high temperatures for extended periods.
Sensitivity to Light and Oxygen: latex is sensitive to both light and oxygen, which can lead to degradation or discoloration over time. This sensitivity might affect the appearance and durability of latex products.
