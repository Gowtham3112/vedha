BENEFITS OF 100% NATURAL LATEX
SAFE
Natural Latex originates from the natural sap of rubber trees, a gift from Mother Nature herself. At Theptex, your health is our top priority, and we stand firm in our commitment to never compromise it—never. We strictly avoid mixing in inferior synthetic rubber (SBR) or any potentially harmful substances that could emit toxic fumes. Your safety and well-being are non-negotiable for us
HEALTH
The natural latex extracted from rubber trees possesses inherent antibacterial properties, which are seamlessly integrated into our natural latex mattresses. Additionally, latex sap's innate characteristics include resistance to dust mites
BENEFITS OF PURE NATURAL LATEX
Harvesting latex from rubber trees doesn't harm the tree, allowing it to sustainably absorb carbon dioxide for years. By using latex in mattresses, we contribute to reducing waste mountains globally. Furthermore, latex mattresses boast superior durability compared to other variants, and when they reach the end of their lifespan, they completely biodegrade, minimizing environmental impact.
SPINE ALIGNMENT
The distinct, non-linear compression pattern of a rubber cell causes areas with more weight from the body to gently sink into the mattress, while the lighter regions continue to receive support. This results in an ideal spine alignment, alleviating discomfort and enhancing blood flow circulation.
RESPIRABLE
Latex mattresses boast a naturally Respirable, open-cell structure that facilitates excellent airflow, effectively maintaining a dry sleeping environment. This unique feature, coupled with latex's antimicrobial properties, ensures these mattresses remain mold and mildew-free, promoting a cooler and more hygienic sleeping experience.
REDUCED MOTION TRANSMISSION
Latex's inherent resilience ensures that motion on one side of the mattress doesn't readily transfer to the opposite side due to its natural properties.
